# Background jobs (src/trigger)

Trigger.dev v4 tasks, configured in `trigger.config.ts`. Specs: [0001](../../docs/specs/0001-stack-architecture/index.md), [0002](../../docs/specs/0002-data-model/index.md), [0004](../../docs/specs/0004-core-take-loop/index.md).

## What each task owns

- `probe`: ffprobe of the original through a presigned URL, the thumbnail (not fatal), `markUploaded` (this is where media failure codes are set), then it starts and dispatches `process-take`. Idempotency key `probe-{takeId}` (global scope), so repeated upload completions never probe twice.
- `process-take`: download, local re probe, silence detection (`-35dB`, 700 ms, in `src/lib/trim.ts`), cuts, render, upload, finish. Key `job-{jobId}`. Payloads carry IDs only.
- `process-take` and backgrounds (spec 0008): a job with `target_background_id` (set by `startJob` from `take.background_id`) has a `background` step. After the trim it loads the background (missing or not `ready` → `BackgroundMissingError` → failure code `background_missing`, refundable), downloads its `image_key`, and `detectInFile` (`src/trigger/keying.ts`) samples 5 frames, tone mapped like the render, and `detectGreenScreen` (`src/lib/keying.ts`) decides. Usable → step `succeeded`, the render keys onto the image (`renderFilterScript(keep, { hdr, hasAudio, background })`, background image as looped input 1, `scale ... out_range=tv` so the output stays limited range). Not usable → step `skipped` with `no_green_screen`; the video keeps its background and renders only if there are cuts. A keyed take always renders, even with no cuts (whole take) or no audio (no audio branch in the graph). The render row snapshots `background_id`, `background_name`, `keyed`, `key_color`. Detection thresholds and the key settings live in `src/lib/keying.ts` (`DETECTION`, `KEYING`) and were tuned on the synthetic fixtures only: re check them on real footage. Measured on 4 vCPUs (the `large-1x` size): a keyed 10 minute 1080p render takes about 15 minutes against 2 for the plain one (chromakey is the cost, about 20 frames per second); with download, trim and upload a worst case job stays near 20 minutes of the 38.5 minute deadline.
- `notify-take` (`src/trigger/email/`): sends the ready or didn't finish email for one finished job (spec 0006). `process-take` queues it after every `finish` through `queueTakeEmail`, which never throws. 3 attempts; `notified_at` and the Resend key `take-email-{jobId}` make a repeat harmless; a bad email env aborts without retrying. Cancelled jobs send nothing.
- Take emails go through `queueTakeEmail(jobId, kind)`, which queues nothing for a kind that is never emailed (`transcribe` and `rerender`: only the first run is emailed, spec 0008), and `notifyTakeFinished` refuses such a job too; every caller passes the job's kind (the cleanup sweep gets it from `StaleJob.kind`). Cleanup's stale loop uses a per kind running cutoff.
- `transcribe-take` (spec 0007): started by `process-take` after every successful initial job (rendered, no cuts or no audio) (`startTranscribeJob`, then `dispatchJob` with `triggerForTranscribe`, key `transcribe-{jobId}`; a throwing trigger finishes the job `trigger_failed`; a refused start only logs). Payload `{ jobId }`; parses `transcriptEnvSchema` itself (`DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`, Trigger.dev env only, a missing key aborts with names only). Presigned original → `probeUrl` → no audio stream skips `no_speech` without calling Deepgram → mono 16 kHz Opus (libopus) in Ogg → plain `fetch` to Deepgram (`src/lib/deepgram.ts`: options incl. `mip_opt_out=true`, zod, `en*` is English) → upsert. 429, 5xx and network retry (3 attempts); other 4xx abort as `provider_rejected`. Skips: `not_english`, `no_speech`, `original_gone`. Final failure codes come from `transcribeFailureCode` (`failTranscribeJob` writes them; the take stays Ready, 0 charged): a 4xx other than 429 is `provider_rejected` (`DeepgramRejected`, status kept on the error), a 5 minute fetch timeout or an attempt past its own deadline is `timeout`, other Deepgram failures `provider_error`. A thrown job start in `process-take` is tried 3 times, then the take shows Make transcript. Never moves the take, charges or emails. Deepgram docs: the `speech-to-text` skill in `.claude/skills/`. Tests inject R2, ffmpeg and Deepgram through `TranscribeIo`.
- `cleanup` (every 15 minutes, UTC): fails abandoned uploads and stale jobs, expires takes, then drains `storage-deletion` (its own queue, concurrency 1; a row that fails 10 times is left for a person).
- `render-spike` is manual only and builds a 10 minute HDR fixture; it has no token guard. `sentry-check` throws inside `run()` only when its payload token matches `SENTRY_CHECK_TOKEN`. `hello` is the setup check.

## Rules worth knowing

- Imports: job code may not import `@/app`, `@/server`, `next`, `react` or `@opennextjs/cloudflare` (ESLint in `eslint.config.mjs`, tested in `src/import-boundaries.test.ts`). Use `src/db/data/*` and `@/lib`.
- ffmpeg is pinned: `pinnedFfmpeg()` in `trigger.config.ts` installs one BtbN build and checks its SHA 256. To upgrade, change the URL and the hash together. Dev runs use the machine's own ffmpeg (`FFMPEG_PATH` and `FFPROBE_PATH` unset fall back to the bare names).
- Render: constant frame rate (`-fps_mode cfr`, `outputFps` in `src/lib/trim.ts`), keep ranges snapped to frames (`alignToFrames`), HDR goes through `toneMapFilter` (zscale needs `tin=<transfer>` spelled out; `media.test.ts` covers it).
- Progress is throttled (`throttledProgress`: at most every 2 s and at least 1 point). Call `flush()` before the final step write. Render progress is scaled to 95.
- Retries: the default is 3 attempts and none in dev. `process-take` has 2 attempts and a 40 minute maximum (`PROCESS_TAKE_LIMITS`); `src/lib/cleanup-rules.ts` derives the stale job cutoff from them, so change both together. `probe` has 3; `sentry-check` and `render-spike` have 1.
- Failure codes are an open set in `src/lib/failure-codes.ts`: add, never rename or remove. `failureCodeFor` maps ffmpeg errors to `ffmpeg_error`, `DeadlineExceeded` (and Trigger's maximum duration) to `timeout` and the rest to `internal_error` (all refundable). A timed out ffmpeg is `ffmpeg_error`, not `timeout`. `finish()` refunds once (compare and swap) and the charge happens at `startJob`, so a retry never charges twice.
- Presigned URLs are secrets. `media.ts` rebuilds tool errors with only the tool name and exit code; never log a URL, key or payload.
- Job deadlines (`src/lib/deadline.ts`): Trigger.dev does not call `onFailure` for a run that exceeds `maxDuration` (seen in a local run: TIMED_OUT, job left active until the stale cleanup marks it `stale_run`). `process-take` and `transcribe-take` therefore wrap their work in `runWithDeadline(deadlineMs(maxDuration))` (stops 90 s early: the failure hooks need up to about 45 s, Sentry flush plus the database connect retries). The signal is passed down: `runFfmpeg` and the ffprobe calls kill the process (and settle only after it exited), `downloadToFile` and the Deepgram fetch abort, `uploadFileMultipart` starts no new part or retry (the back off wakes on the signal) and aborts the multipart upload (a part already in flight finishes), and each task's own `finally` removes its temp directory. After that `DeadlineExceeded` is thrown from inside `run()`, `failureCodeFor` maps it to `timeout` and the normal failure path finishes the job (refunded once for `process-take`; a transcribe job leaves the take Ready). A timed out attempt must not write after the deadline: `process-take` checks the signal before it commits a success, `runWithDeadline` rejects a late success, and its cut and render writes go through `whileJobActive` (`src/db/data/jobs.ts`: locks the take, then the job, and rolls back unless the job is still queued or running), like `saveTranscriptAndFinish` for the transcript. New long work must take the signal and clean up in a `finally`; stale cleanup stays the backstop for a crashed worker. `probe` needs no deadline: its tool calls have their own 60 s limits (at most about 4 of them, under its 300 s) and the stuck upload recovery covers an overrun.
- Sentry: `jobFailureTags` adds `providerStatus` when the error has a numeric `status` (a refused Deepgram request, so a spending cap hit shows its 402), never the message. `init.ts` starts with no default integrations, and a global `tasks.onFailure` sends the allowlisted tags (`jobFailureTags`) and flushes. A payload that fails its schema never reaches `onFailure`, so test failures from inside `run()`. `process-take` and `probe` also have their own `onFailure` that writes database state.
- Tests: `media.test.ts` fakes ffprobe by stubbing `FFPROBE_PATH`; the path is read at module load, so reset modules before importing.
