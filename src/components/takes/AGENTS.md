# Take pages and upload (src/components/takes)

The library at `/library` (`/` redirects signed in visitors there) and the take page at `/takes/[id]`. Specs: [0004 core take loop](../../../docs/specs/0004-core-take-loop/index.md). UI rules: `design.md`.

## How it is split

- Pure rules live in `src/lib`, not here: `take-display.ts` (status badges, mm:ss, expiry notes, weighted progress, tab titles, poll delay), `take-api.ts` (zod shapes for the take routes), `upload-client.ts` (the whole upload flow, with its I/O passed in so tests can fake it), `upload-rules.ts`.
- `browser-io.ts` is the browser side of that I/O: `callApi` (30 second timeout on every call) and `putPart` (XMLHttpRequest, because fetch has no upload progress). Keep new upload calls bounded the same way.
- Components are client components. Polling goes through `use-poll.ts` (pauses while the tab is hidden, backs off on errors). `use-now.ts` is the library's clock, so expiry notes stay true on a page left open.

## Rules worth knowing

- Failure wording: media failures use `src/lib/take-messages.ts` and `src/lib/failure-codes.ts`. Do not copy those into this folder. Our own failures are worded in `take-display.ts`.
- Failed takes: if `take.retryable` show "Try again"; otherwise show "Upload another video", and `failureMessage(take, retryable)` words it to match.
- Expiry: show `effectiveStatus(...)`, not the raw status, so a ready take past its expiry shows as expired. Dates are UTC. Under a day left reads "Video expires in less than a day", never "today" or "tomorrow".
- Thumbnails come from `/api/takes/{id}/media?kind=thumb` and keep the placeholder when there is none. A card re-asks when its status changes.
- Pages read the session through `getSession()` in `src/server/request.ts`.
- Transcript (spec 0007): `transcript-panel.tsx` composes the states (making, ready, no words, skipped with a reason, failed, none); `use-transcript.ts` reads and polls GET `/api/takes/{id}/transcript` and starts the job (a start message stays until a job this page saw making has finished; no "we'll keep trying" after a 401 or 404; a load error shows beside a loaded view); `transcript-paragraphs.tsx` and `transcript-downloads.tsx` are the words and the two download links. Fixes (spec 0012, S5A-T05): `transcript-fixes.tsx` lists the visible fixes with Undo or Redo (PATCH `/transcript/edits/{index}` through `setEditUndone` in `use-transcript.ts`, which changes the view only on a 200 and takes the new `version` from the reply so the caption track reloads); fixed words are underlined solid (fillers stay dotted); pure helpers in `src/lib/transcript-fixes.ts`. The panel and the player share `player-bridge.ts`, a small store (seek, current time, caption track version) read with `useSyncExternalStore`, so a time update re-renders only the panel's paragraph marker. The caption `<track>` comes from the VTT route with `?v={version}` and the CC toggle (`use-captions.ts`) keeps the track `hidden` when off; the video's `poster` is the `thumb` media link (`use-poster.ts`). `seekTo` keeps the time when the duration is still unknown, so a click before the metadata loads still seeks.
- `background-change.tsx` (spec 0008, spec 0011 AC-5): the picker section on a ready take: grid, preview on the stage, notes and the clean edges tip. A pick does not render: it calls `review.pickBackground`, joins the pending bar and Render there makes the video (`POST /changes`). A background that was deleted reads as Original. Suggested card (spec 0012 AC-5, S5A-T07): `suggested-background.tsx` shows "Suggested: <name>", the AI's reason and "Use it" above the grid when the take GET `suggestion` is set; `showSuggestion` hides it when `suggestion` is null, the take has no green screen, or it is already the current background. "Use it" is the same `review.pickBackground` as a tile pick (pending bar, then Render), and the card then says it was added; nothing is applied by itself. The current and the chosen background stay visible when the list fails (`selectOptions` in `src/lib/background-options.ts`).
- Background choice at upload (spec 0008): `use-backgrounds.ts` reads GET `/api/backgrounds`; `background-select.tsx` is the upload area's compact select and wraps `background-picker.tsx` (a dialog, a bottom sheet on phones, with the trigger passed in so Radix returns focus to it) made of `background-tile.tsx` radios. The upload sends `backgroundId` only once the list has loaded: the creator's pick, else their saved `defaultId`, else `original`; a failed read sends nothing. Tests: `tests/background-picker.spec.ts`.
- The background picker (spec 0008, AC-3 to AC-6, AC-11, AC-12): `background-grid.tsx` is the one set of tiles (Keep original, Studios, extras that must stay visible, Your backgrounds with the upload tile), used by the dialog in the upload area (`background-picker.tsx`) and by the take page section (`background-change.tsx`). The section picks a background, asks for a preview (`use-background-preview.ts`: POST then checks every 2 s for about 90 s; the answer is derived per choice, so a new choice abandons the old checks) and shows it over the stage through the player bridge (`setPreview`, rendered by `background-preview-overlay.tsx` inside the player's letterbox, while the video is paused under it, see the next line); "Keep original" shows the take's own thumbnail. The change itself goes through the pending bar (`use-change-render.ts`). `background-notes.tsx` has the notes and the clean edges tip. A deleted background leaves the cached list and the default at once (`forget` in `use-backgrounds.ts`), and a pick made while the delete was pending is kept. The current and the chosen background stay visible when the list fails (`selectOptions` in `src/lib/background-options.ts` supplies the extras). Progress is text only: the take detail has no step progress for a background change.
- Uploading a background from the picker (spec 0008, AC-8, AC-10): `use-background-upload.ts` checks the type and size, POSTs `/api/backgrounds`, PUTs the file to the presigned link with its content type, completes, then polls the list (2 s, about a minute) for `ready` (selects it) or `failed` (the friendly message for `failureCode`, from `background-rules`); `background-mine.tsx` is the "Your backgrounds" section (ready tiles with Delete, the Upload image tile, a line for each image still processing or refused). A 409 `in_use` shows the server's message. Minimal on purpose; the take page picker (part 6) builds on it.
- Take page after a background change (S3S-T13): `take-view.tsx` keeps checking a ready take (`keepPolling` in `take-display.ts`: every 2 s while a job runs, every `POLL.idleMs` 15 s when idle, and at once when the tab becomes visible), so a second tab or a page left open sees a change finished elsewhere. `TakePlayer` gets `renderId` (`detail.latestRender.id`): when it changes the player fetches the new link and swaps `src` on the same `<video>`, resuming the position and the paused or playing state (read just before the swap; if the new link cannot be fetched the current video stays, with two quiet retries and then a "Try again" line); Download and Download original ask the server when clicked, so they always give the latest render. A refused change shows its message in the pending bar's status line (`CHANGE_MESSAGES`), and a 409 `stale` also reads the take again. `use-poster.ts` returns `{ url, refresh }`: the thumbnail link is fetched again when the tab is shown after 30 minutes and when an image fails to load (at most once per 10 s); `background-tile.tsx` shows its placeholder instead of a broken image, and "Keep original" shows the take's thumbnail on the take page.
- Still preview on the stage (S3S-T14): while `usePreviewOverlay` has a preview (the still or "making"), `take-player.tsx` pauses the video, hides the transport (the controls stay mounted but `hidden` and `inert`, shortcuts off) and shows a `role="status"` line (`previewStatusText` in `take-display.ts`: "Still preview, not a video. Press \u201cRender\u201d to make it.") plus a "Back to your video" button. `bridge.back()` calls what `background-change.tsx` registered with `bridge.setBack` (pick the current background again, so the preview ends). A layout effect moves focus to that button when it was inside the controls; the status region is always in the page so the line is announced. The overlay no longer lets clicks through to the video.
- Cut review (spec 0011, S4C-T03): `use-cut-review.ts` holds the page's edits (only what differs from the take GET; a reload drops edits that became redundant, `normalizeEdits`) and feeds `cut-timeline.tsx` (width `max(container, 8 px per second)`, `cut-block.tsx` numbered toggle buttons with `aria-pressed` and a 24 by 44 px hit area, `waveform-track.tsx` or a plain track, the summary line, then a polite `role="status"` reason line for the focused or last toggled cut (`reasonLine` in `src/lib/cut-review.ts`, "Cut 4: reason"; each block points `aria-describedby` at an `sr-only` span holding its reason; spec 0012 AC-3), and "AI edit wasn't available for this take." when the take GET `aiEdit` is `"unavailable"` (never for null)) and `pending-bar.tsx` (changes in order, Discard, Render, or Try again after a failed render; a status region present from the first load; under 640 px the text has the full width and the two buttons share the row below it, 44 px tall). `use-change-render.ts` sends `baseRenderId`, follows its own `jobId` through `GET /jobs/{id}` and shows refusal messages; another tab's job locks the page and keeps the pending edits. While cuts are pending the player previews the original (`use-original-source.ts`: fetch the link, real playability probe, 410 means gone, link refreshed once on a media error) and skips the pending cuts per frame (`src/lib/cut-playback.ts`); `use-source-switch.ts` keeps the position across the `src` change and maps the playhead through `latestRender.removedRanges`; `player-notice.tsx` words the fallbacks. `use-waveform.ts` reads `GET /waveform` once per load. While the original plays the caption track is `transcript/vtt?timeline=original` (cues on the original timeline), and `use-render-link.ts` keeps the removed ranges of the shown render (`shownRanges`) so a swap maps the position through the old and the new cuts. `use-leave-guard.ts` asks before a reload, an in app link or Back loses unsent edits. Tests: `tests/cut-review.spec.ts` (includes the failed render path, spec 0011 AC-6: the changes stay, Try again resends the same body, a reload loses them), `tests/background-take-page.spec.ts`.
- Take page layout (spec 0009 AC-5, design.md §4b): `take-view.tsx` lays out the header (title, Rename, length, expiry), then the dark stage panel (`TakeStage`: player with a glass transport, `Stage` has the 4% grain) over the summary row (`take-result.tsx`: "Removed ..." line, "Download", "Download original") on the left, and `take-inspector.tsx` on the right (stacked under 1200 px). The inspector has pill tabs Export, Background, Transcript, Details for a ready or expired take, and only Details otherwise. Background and Transcript use `forceMount` (hidden, not removed), so a studio preview on the stage, the transcript's polling and seek keep working while another tab shows; the Export tab and the Transcript tab share one `useTranscript` read. Export rows use "Save" buttons with their own names (`Save edited video`, `Save original video`, `Save captions (SRT)`, `Save transcript (TXT)`) so they never clash with the summary row; `use-take-downloads.ts` is the one download link flow. Details only shows what the take data carries (no resolution or size yet, no delete: slice 6). The take page publishes its live title and status with `usePublishCurrentTake`, so the top bar's breadcrumb and chip follow a rename and processing to ready. Tests open the Background or Transcript panel by clicking its tab first (`tests/background-take-page.spec.ts` `open`, `tests/transcript.spec.ts` `openTranscript`).
- Browser tests: `tests/takes.spec.ts`, with helpers in `tests/support/takes.ts`.
