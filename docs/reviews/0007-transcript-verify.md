## Worth knowing

- A transcribe job also starts when a take is Ready with no cuts (the original is kept), not only after a rendered video (noted on #83). That matches AC-1, which says "an initial job succeeds".
- The SRT and VTT content types carry `; charset=utf-8`, which the spec does not list; it keeps non ASCII captions readable.
- Locally, the first browser test after a cold server start sometimes times out and passes on rerun; CI retries once. If it ever shows up there, a warm up request is the fix.
- Slice 4 will show removed words struck through; until then the panel shows only words in the trimmed video, as the spec says.
