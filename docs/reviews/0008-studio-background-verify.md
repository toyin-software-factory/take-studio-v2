# Verify: studio backgrounds, spec 0008

**Verdict: not closed. Ten of the twelve acceptance criteria are met with evidence. AC-2 and AC-4 FAIL on real footage (tracked as S3S-T10).** Toyin made a real green screen take on production on 2026-11-12 and the background preview keyed the person out entirely. My synthetic fixtures, and the earlier runs I cite, all passed, so the report's earlier PASS for AC-2 and AC-4 held only for synthetic footage. Two wording gaps (AC-4 and AC-6) were closed by the lead updating the spec to the app's shipped messages (#113); AC-7 was confirmed live by Toyin. Scope feature 8 is back to in progress and spec 0008 back to In Progress; the feature closes after S3S-T10 and a new verify of AC-2 and AC-4 on real footage.

Run on 2026-11-12 against main at `1b99c04` (after #111, the six studios): a local build of that commit served by the preview Worker, a local Postgres, and seeded test users only (no real account, and nothing on production: I had no production address to call, see "Owed"). I also cite the real runs on earlier PRs of this slice (real R2, real Trigger.dev dev, real ffmpeg). I read those, I did not repeat them: this sandbox only has placeholder R2 keys. No personal data and no secrets are in this report. Fixtures are synthetic shapes and Toyin's approved studio images.

## Gaps found, and how they were closed

1. **AC-4, the no green screen message.** The spec wrote "This take has no green screen, so backgrounds can't be applied"; the app says "We couldn't find a green screen in this take, so this background wouldn't show." for a preview and "We couldn't find a green screen in this take, so we kept the original background." as the page note. Closed by #113: the spec now carries the app's wording (checked against `background-notes.tsx` and `take-display.ts`).
2. **AC-6, the 429 message.** The spec wrote "You've changed backgrounds 10 times this hour. Try again in a little while."; the app says "You've changed backgrounds a lot in the last hour. Please try again later." Closed by #113 (checked against `src/server/backgrounds/http.ts`).
3. **AC-2 and AC-4 on real footage (open, S3S-T10).** A real green screen take on production had the person keyed out entirely in the background preview. Found by Toyin after the first pass; not reproduced by me. Needs a fix and a new verify of AC-2 and AC-4 on real footage (and a keyed render, not only the preview).

## Worth knowing

- The hourly limit counts jobs through their take, so deleting a take lowers a creator's count. That is a recorded lead decision (spec 0008 follow ups, the slice 6 usage ledger), not a gap here.
- The place the sandbox is weakest is the player: the seeded takes have no real video file, so screenshots show "The video didn't load" in the stage. The picker and the notes are what the screenshots are for.
- This report names the job a background change starts as a `rerender` job, as main does today. #110 and HRF-T08 move it to a separate `background` job kind; that is pending work, not a gap, and the behaviour checks above do not depend on the kind name.
- Why this verify missed it: every keying check I ran or cited uses synthetic clips (evenly lit shapes on one green). Real footage has uneven light, shadows and skin tones the synthetic clips do not. The verify gate for this feature should include a real green screen take before it closes.
