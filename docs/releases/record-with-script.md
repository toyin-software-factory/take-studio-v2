# Record with a script

You can now record a take inside Take Studio, on your phone or laptop, while a teleprompter shows your script right next to the lens. When you stop, the take goes straight into the normal flow: transcript, cuts, background and download.

## How it works

- Open Record, paste your script (optional) and turn on your camera. Choose your camera and microphone if you have more than one, and flip the camera on a phone.
- The prompter scrolls at a speed you set. Pause it, make it faster or slower, and change the text size with the buttons or the keys P, up and down arrows, plus and minus. Your settings are remembered on your device.
- Turn on Follow my voice and the prompter moves to where you are in the script, even if you skip a line or add your own words. If it cannot hear your place it goes back to a timed scroll and tells you.
- A recording stops by itself at 10 minutes, or sooner if your monthly allowance would run out. A timer always shows what is left.
- Play your take back, then choose Use this take, Record again or Save to your device. If an upload fails, the recording stays so you can try again.

## Say a line again

If you fluff a line, just say it again. When a take has a script, the AI keeps your best attempt and cuts the others.

## Your privacy

- Your script is saved with the take, shown on its Details tab, and deleted when you delete the take or your account.
- With Follow my voice on, your microphone audio goes to Deepgram while you record. It is not stored, and a first time dialog explains this before it turns on.
- Nothing leaves your device until you choose Use this take.
