# 0008. Studio background swap

**Date**: 2026-11-12
**Status**: Accepted

## Summary

Creators filmed on a green screen get it replaced with a studio background: one of six built in studios, or their own uploaded image. The background is picked before upload (remembered for next time) and can be changed on the take page, where a still preview shows the take on each background before a re-render. The cut out runs in our existing video job with ffmpeg (chroma key plus green spill removal), automatically and at no extra cost; takes without a usable green screen keep their original background with a clear note. Changing the background later re-renders only the video, keeps the current one playable meanwhile, and costs no minutes.

## Context

The product promise is "pauses trimmed, green screen swapped for a studio background, and a transcript". Slices 1 and 2 shipped the first and last parts. Scope feature 8 asks that a creator picks a preset or uploads an image (format and size checked), and that the finished video shows the subject cleanly on that background with no visible green edges in normal lighting.

Forces:
- **Stack.** Spec 0001 chose ffmpeg chroma key plus overlay inside the Trigger.dev video job (free, proven), with AI matting as a possible later upgrade.
- **Today's job code.** `startJob` derives `initial` or `rerender` itself and always moves the take to `processing`; `finishInTx` always transitions the take; `process-take` skips rendering when a take has no audio or no applied cuts; `EMAIL_JOB_KINDS` includes `rerender`. Spec 0007 added the precedent of a separate start and finish path (`startTranscribeJob`) for a job that leaves the take Ready.
- **Storage.** `storage_deletion` deletes exact keys (no prefix delete); R2 presigned PUTs can pin an exact length, not a range.
- **Lighting varies.** Chroma key needs an even green; poor light and green reflected onto hair (spill) cause edges; keying a take with no green screen produces a broken mix.
- **Web app limits.** The Worker never handles file bytes and may not import the Trigger.dev SDK internals beyond triggering; image checks happen in jobs.
- **Answers from Toyin (2026-11-12):** green screen only for now; pick before upload (remembered) and change any time after; detect a missing green screen and keep the original with a note; six built in studios generated by Toyin with ChatGPT or Gemini from the lead's prompts, each approved; upload rules (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 10 MB, at least 1280 by 720, fill and centre crop, private library with delete); a quick still preview before re-rendering; fully automatic keying with a "Getting clean edges" tip; data plan accepted; no references; the cross check's fixes applied.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As a creator filmed on a green screen, I want my finished video on a studio background so it looks professional without editing.
- As a creator, I want to pick my usual studio once and have new takes use it.
- As a creator, I want to try other backgrounds on a take and see a preview before waiting for a render.
- As a creator, I want to use my own background image.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract):
- **AC-1**: The upload area has a "Background" choice (built in studios, the creator's ready uploads, and "Keep original"), preselected with the creator's last pick (default "Keep original"; a deleted or not ready pick falls back to it). Starting an upload stores the choice on the take and as the creator's new default.
- **AC-2**: When a take with a chosen background is processed, the finished video shows the subject on that background, scaled to fill the frame and centre cropped for the video's own shape (no bars, no stretching), with green spill removed; in an evenly lit green screen fixture no pixel near the subject's outline stays visibly green. A take with a chosen background is always rendered, even with no pauses or no sound. Trim, timing, sound and transcript behave exactly as without a background.
- **AC-3**: When the take has no usable green screen, the background step ends `skipped` with reason `no_green_screen`, the video keeps its original background, and the take page says "We couldn't find a green screen in this take, so we kept the original background." The take is Ready as normal and the charge is unchanged.
- **AC-4**: On a Ready take, the take page shows the background picker. Selecting a background shows a still preview of one frame of the take on it, typically within about 20 seconds (cached per take, background and cut version); "Original" shows the take's thumbnail at once. If the take has no green screen, the picker says "We couldn't find a green screen in this take, so this background wouldn't show." (on the preview; the take page note reads "We couldn't find a green screen in this take, so we kept the original background.") and previews show the original frame. A keyed preview whose centre third differs from the plain background in under 2% of pixels (the person was keyed away) is treated the same way: the original frame with the no green screen note, never a Ready still of only the background (S3S-T12). "Use this background" starts a re-render; "Keep original" re-renders without a background. The current background is marked. *Updated by spec 0011 (AC-5): selecting a background (or "Original") now adds it to the take page's pending changes bar, and Render there applies it together with any cut changes in one job; there is no separate "Use this background" render.*
- **AC-5**: A background re-render keeps the take Ready with its current video playable, reuses the take's current cuts (no new trim), charges 0 seconds, keeps the transcript, sends no email, and shows render progress. On success the new video and background become current; on failure the previous video and background stay and the page offers "Try again".
- **AC-6**: Changing the background is refused with a friendly message while another job runs for the take ("Your transcript is still being made. Try again in a moment." when it is a transcript), when the take is not Ready, when its original has expired, or when the chosen background is not ready. A creator can start at most 10 background changes per hour ("You've changed backgrounds a lot in the last hour. Please try again later.", 429). On a take whose latest render found no green screen, choosing a background is refused and the button is disabled with the no green screen note; switching to "Keep original" stays allowed. *Updated by spec 0011 (AC-8): the limit is shared by every change to a Ready take (cuts and background together, 10 per creator per hour, HTTP error `change_limit`, "You've made a lot of changes in the last hour. Please try again later."), and the request goes through `POST /api/takes/[id]/changes`.*
- **AC-7**: Six built in studios are available to every signed in creator, each with a name, a 16:9 thumbnail and alt text, generated from the approved prompts and approved by Toyin before shipping, and installed in every environment by a script.
- **AC-8**: A creator can upload a background (JPEG, PNG or WebP; at most 10 MB; at least 1280 by 720 and at most 40 megapixels; not animated). Other files are refused with a clear message (format, size, too small, too large or animated). Transparent areas become black. Accepted images appear in the creator's library with a thumbnail and can be reused and deleted.
- **AC-9**: Uploaded backgrounds are private: only their owner sees, previews, uses or deletes them; another creator's background ID returns not found. Built in studios cannot be deleted or changed through the app.
- **AC-10**: Deleting an uploaded background removes it from the library and queues all its files for deletion; it is refused ("In use, try again in a minute") while a job is rendering with it. Takes that used it keep their finished video and show its name from the render snapshot; their next re-render defaults to "Keep original". *Updated by spec 0011 (AC-8): on such a take the pending changes bar starts with "Background: Original", and a change request that names no background is refused with `background_missing`.* Deleting a take deletes its preview stills; deleting an account deletes its uploaded backgrounds and their files.
- **AC-11**: The take page shows a short "Getting clean edges" tip near the picker (even light, no shadows on the green, stand a step away from it).
- **AC-12**: The picker follows `design.md` (16:9 thumbnail grid, built ins first, selected item ringed with a check) and WCAG AA: keyboard selection, visible focus, alt text, 44 px targets, works at phone width, and axe finds no serious or critical issues.
- **AC-13**: A finished keyed render is checked before it is stored: one frame of the output at its middle is compared with the background in the centre third, with the same gate as a preview (AC-4: under 2% of pixels differing means the person was keyed away). A render that is only the background is never shipped: the video is rendered again without the key (original background, the same cuts), the render row records `keyed` false with no key colour (its background name stays, so the take page shows the AC-3 note "We couldn't find a green screen in this take, so we kept the original background."), and the background step ends `skipped` with `no_green_screen`. The extra render is free (no extra charge) and sends no extra email; logs carry IDs only. (RF3-T05)

## Options considered

### Option 1: ffmpeg chroma key with automatic tuning in the video job

Detect the green screen and its exact shade from sampled frames, then key with ffmpeg `chromakey` plus `despill`, scale and crop the background, and overlay, inside the existing render pass.

**Pros**:
- Free per take, no new vendor, video never leaves our stack.
- One render pass: no extra encode, no quality loss.

**Cons**:
- Quality depends on the creator's lighting.
- Thresholds need tuning with real footage; renders get slower.

### Option 2: AI background removal (matting model on a GPU provider)

Send the video to a hosted matting model that separates the person without a green screen.

**Pros**:
- Works without a green screen; better on uneven light and hair.

**Cons**:
- A new paid vendor, video sent to a third party, slower; Trigger.dev has no GPUs. Toyin chose green screen only for now.

### Option 3: Key in the browser

Do the cut out client side with WebGL.

**Pros**:
- Instant previews.

**Cons**:
- Downloads still need a server render; two keying implementations to keep identical; heavy on phones.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1: ffmpeg chroma key with automatic tuning in the video job.

Initial runs key inside `process-take`'s render; background changes use their own `background` job kind (added by HRF-T08 after the 2026-11-12 outside review; first shipped as `rerender`) through a new `startBackgroundJob` and finish branch that leave the take Ready (the spec 0007 transcribe pattern), with the target background recorded on the job.

## Rationale

Toyin's choice (green screen only) and spec 0001's plan meet here: ffmpeg already runs in our job, costs nothing per take and keeps video private. Edge quality is the real risk, handled by detecting the actual green from the take, spill removal, an honest skip when the green is not usable, and the lighting tip. AI matting stays a clean later upgrade: it would replace `src/lib/keying.ts`, not the data model or UI.

Smaller calls made here:
- **Detection from the frame's border.** Sample 5 frames between 10 % and 90 % of the take, after the same HDR to SDR tone map the render uses. Measure 16 patches (each 4 % of width by 4 % of height) along the left, right and top edges. A patch is green when its median HSV has hue 80 to 160 degrees, saturation at least 0.30 and value at least 0.25. Usable when at least 10 of 16 patches are green in at least 3 of 5 frames; the key colour is the median RGB of all green patches. Runner up: whole frame pixel coverage, which green clothing, plants or a narrow backdrop fool more easily.
- **YUV `chromakey` plus `despill`** (not `colorkey` alone): handles brightness changes on the green and removes the green cast on hair. `similarity` and `blend` start at 0.12 and 0.08, tuned with fixtures in step 1.
- **Background images kept large, cropped per take.** Ingest stores a normalised JPEG no larger than 3840 by 2160 (aspect kept) plus a 480 by 270 WebP thumbnail; each render scales to cover and centre crops that image to the take's own width, height and orientation, so portrait and 4K takes stay sharp. Runner up: one fixed 1920 by 1080 copy (blurry on portrait and 4K).
- **Previews from one seeked frame.** The preview task asks ffmpeg to seek (HTTP range) to the thumbnail's time in the original instead of downloading it, keys that frame and writes a 1280 wide JPEG; one preview at a time per take (queue concurrency key `preview-{takeId}`).
- **Re-renders send no email.** The creator starts them from the take page and sees the result there.

## Feature design

**Data model sketch** (target; spec 0002's plan plus additions marked new):

| Table | Fields | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `background` (new table, planned in 0002) | `id`; `creator_id` (null = built in); `name` (1 to 80); `source_key` (uploaded file, null for built ins, new); `image_key` (normalised JPEG, at most 3840x2160); `thumb_key` (480x270 WebP, new); `width`, `height` (of the normalised image); `bytes` (declared upload size, new); `status` (`uploading`, `processing`, `ready`, `failed`, new; built ins `ready`); `failure_code` (null); `alt` (built ins; uploads use their name); timestamps | FK `creator_id` → `user` CASCADE; index (`creator_id`, `created_at`); index (`status`, `created_at`) |
| `background_preview` (new) | `id`; `take_id`; `background_id` (required; "Original" previews never use this table); `cut_version` (hash of the applied cut ranges); `status` (`making`, `ready`, `failed`); `object_key` (null until ready); `no_green_screen` boolean; timestamps | FK `take_id` → `take` CASCADE; FK `background_id` → `background` CASCADE; unique (`take_id`, `background_id`, `cut_version`) |
| `take` | `background_id` (the current background, null = original) | FK → `background` ON DELETE SET NULL; index. Changed only by upload create and by a successful background re-render |
| `processing_job` | `target_background_id` (new; the background a job renders, null = original) | FK → `background` ON DELETE SET NULL; set at job start from the take (initial) or the request (background) |
| `render` | `background_id`, `background_name` (snapshot at render start); `keyed` boolean (new; true only when the green was actually replaced); `key_color` (new; hex, null when not keyed); a keyed render found to be only the background (AC-13) is redone and the same row set `keyed` false, `key_color` null before upload | FK `background_id` ON DELETE SET NULL; name kept when the background is deleted |
| `user` | `default_background_id` (new; null = keep original) | plain Drizzle column on Better Auth's `user` table (not an `additionalFields` entry; never written through Better Auth; always read from the database, not the session cache); FK ON DELETE SET NULL |
| `job_step` | `background` step goes live; skip reason `no_green_screen` | `SKIP_REASONS` gains `no_green_screen` |

Files (R2; the database stores keys only): uploads `backgrounds/{creatorId}/{backgroundId}/source`, `.../image.jpg`, `.../thumb.webp`; built ins `backgrounds/builtin/{slug}.jpg` and `.webp`; previews `previews/{takeId}/{previewId}.jpg`. Every file is deleted by exact key through `storage_deletion`: a background's three keys on delete or account deletion, a take's preview keys (read from `background_preview`) on take deletion.

Registries and codes: `LIVE_SLICE = 3`; `STEPS_BY_KIND.initial` = `trim`, `background`, `render` with `background` created only when the take has a background (`stepsFor(kind, { hasBackground })`); `STEPS_BY_KIND.background` = `background` (only when the target is a background), `render`; `EMAIL_JOB_KINDS` = `initial` only (success and failure paths, including `process-take`'s `onFailure`); failure codes add `background_missing` (job, refundable: true; the image vanished mid job), `image_invalid`, `image_too_small`, `image_too_large`, `image_animated` (ingest only, refundable: false).

**Lock order** (extends the rule in `src/db/AGENTS.md`): creator, background, take, job, then renders, cuts, steps, previews. `startBackgroundJob`, upload create and `createRender` lock the background row `FOR KEY SHARE` before the take; background delete locks the background `FOR UPDATE` first, then the takes and jobs that reference it.

**State transitions**:
- `background.status`: `uploading → processing → ready | failed` (`/complete` moves to `processing`; ingest decides). Rows still `uploading` or `processing` after 1 hour are failed by cleanup, which queues their keys.
- Background re-render: take stays `ready`; `background` job `queued → running → succeeded | failed`. Success (in one transaction): the new render becomes latest (`flipLatestRender`), `take.background_id` = the job's `target_background_id`. Failure: the job is `failed` with its code; the take, its latest render and `take.background_id` are unchanged.
- `background_preview.status`: `making → ready | failed`; a `failed` or stale row is replaced by a new request. Stale means `making` for longer than 8 minutes (`PREVIEW_STALE_MS` in `src/lib/background-preview.ts`): the preview task's `maxDuration` times its attempts (2 × 180 s) plus 2 minutes for waiting in its queue, so a preview still queued or running is never duplicated.

**Job seam** (modelled on spec 0007's `startTranscribeJob`):
- `startJob` (initial, unchanged trigger) sets `target_background_id` from `take.background_id` and creates the `background` step only when it is set.
- New `startBackgroundJob(tx, { takeId, backgroundId | null })`: lock the creator row first (for the limit), then background (key share), then take; refuse `background_change_limit` when the creator already started 10 `background` jobs in the last hour (counted from `processing_job` joined to `take.creator_id`; the limit lives in `src/lib`); refuse `no_green_screen` when the target is a background and the latest render has `keyed = false` with a `background_name`; require `take.status = ready`, `video_expires_at` null or in the future, no active job (the existing index), and the background built in or owned and `ready`; refuse `same_background` when the target equals the latest render's effective background (`keyed ? background_id : null`) and the latest job did not fail; insert a `background` job with 0 seconds, `target_background_id`, and its steps; no take transition.
- `finishInTx` branch for `background` jobs: no take transition, no refund; on success flip latest and set `take.background_id`; on failure leave everything.
- Moving to the `background` kind (HRF-T08) is backward compatible. The migration only widens the `processing_job.kind` CHECK to also allow `background` (a superset, so old code keeps working) and converts no row: the old "no `trim` step" rule cannot tell a background change from a `startJob` re render, because a `rerender` never has a `trim` step row, so nothing persisted separates them. Old background changes stay `rerender` rows and are not converted. The temporary handling of a `rerender` as a possible background change (RF3-T02 removed it) is gone: a `rerender` is only a re render of a failed take, never a background change, and does not count toward the hourly limit. The new code creates only `background` jobs.
- `job_active` refusals look up the active job's kind so a running `transcribe` job gets the transcript message.

**Pipeline**:
1. `process-take`, kind `initial`: trim as today. If the job has a `target_background_id`: load the background (missing or not ready → `background_missing`), run detection; not usable → step `skipped` `no_green_screen` and render as today (but render even with no cuts or no audio, using `keepRanges = [whole take]` when there are no cuts); usable → step `succeeded`, render with keying. The render row snapshots `background_id`, `background_name`, `keyed` and `key_color`.
2. `process-take`, kind `background`: download the original as today; skip the trim and `reanalyse`; build keep ranges from the take's applied cuts with the same `keepRanges(...)` and `alignToFrames(...)` the initial render used (whole take when none); background step as in 1 for the job's target (none for original); render; flip latest and set `take.background_id` on success; never charge, never email.
3. Render filter chain (`renderFilterScript(keep, { hdr, background })`): trim and concat the kept ranges once; tone map as today; then, when keyed, `chromakey=0x{key}:{similarity}:{blend},despill=type=green` on the concatenated video, the background input opened with `-loop 1 -framerate {outputFps}`, scaled to cover and cropped to the take's display size, `overlay=format=auto:shortest=1`, output `yuv420p` (10 bit inputs keyed in `yuva444p` then converted). Audio unchanged.
4. `preview-background` task (payload `{ previewId }`; 2 attempts; 3 minute (180 s) maximum per attempt; concurrency key `preview-{takeId}` limit 1): ffmpeg seeks to the thumbnail's time through the presigned original URL (no full download), tone maps, runs detection on that frame (or reuses the latest render's `key_color` when `keyed`), keys and composites at 1280 width, writes the JPEG, sets `ready` (or `ready` with `no_green_screen` and the plain frame). Before writing it re-reads the row and take; if either is gone it writes nothing.
5. `ingest-background` task (payload `{ backgroundId }`; 3 attempts; 5 minute maximum): HEAD the source (size above `bytes` or 10 MiB → `image_too_large`); ffprobe: codec in `mjpeg`, `png`, `webp` else `image_invalid`; more than one frame → `image_animated`; below 1280x720 → `image_too_small`; above 40 megapixels → `image_too_large`; then decode the first frame (EXIF rotation applied, alpha flattened on black, converted to sRGB 8 bit), write `image.jpg` (fit within 3840x2160) and `thumb.webp` (480x270 cover crop), set `ready`. Before writing it re-reads the row; if deleted, it writes nothing and queues the source key.
6. `scripts/seed-backgrounds.ts` (idempotent): uploads the six built in images and thumbnails from `assets/backgrounds/` to R2 and upserts their rows with fixed IDs, names and alt text; run by the deploy workflow after migrate and by `pnpm db:seed` locally.

**Key invariants**:
- One active job per take (existing index); a background change never starts while any job runs.
- `take.background_id` changes only at upload create or with a successful background re-render, so it always matches the latest render's intent.
- A take's playable video is always the latest succeeded render; a failed re-render never removes it.
- A creator only ever sees, previews, uses or deletes built ins and their own backgrounds; only `ready` ones can be used.
- Re-renders charge 0 seconds and send no email; the initial job's charge does not depend on the background.
- Files are deleted by exact key only; every stored key is reachable from a row until queued.
- Payloads, logs and Sentry carry IDs and numbers only; never images, frames, file keys or links.

**Security model**: Session scoped as spec 0002: backgrounds by `creator_id` (built ins readable by all signed in creators, writable by none through the app), takes by `take.creator_id`. Presigned PUTs sign content type and exact length; the ingest task, not the browser, decides validity, and caps pixels to protect the job from decompression bombs. Uploaded images are personal data: private, deleted with the account (`beforeDeleteUser` queues each background's keys) or on request. Previews are deleted with their take.

**Configuration required**: none new (R2, Trigger.dev and the pinned ffmpeg already exist). The deploy workflow gains the seed step.

**UI**:
- Upload area: a compact "Background" select showing the current pick's thumbnail and name; opens the picker in a dialog (bottom sheet on phones).
- Take page: a "Background" section under the player with the picker grid (built ins first, then "Your backgrounds" with an "Upload image" tile), the preview shown on the stage when a tile is selected, "Use this background" and "Keep original", render progress reusing the processing steps while the current video keeps playing, the skip note (AC-3), the no green screen note (AC-4), "Try again" after a failed re-render, and the "Getting clean edges" tip (AC-11).
- Mobbin references for background pickers are pulled before building and linked in the PR and `design.md`.

**Critical test scenarios**:
- Green screen fixtures (evenly lit, synthetic person shape on #00B140; 1080p landscape, portrait, 10 bit HDR; one with no pauses and one with no audio): finished video keyed, no outline pixel in the green band after despill, CFR and sound intact, transcript timings unchanged, verifies **AC-2**.
- No green screen fixtures (no green, dark patchy green, green shirt only, a narrow backdrop): step skipped `no_green_screen`, original kept, same charge, verifies **AC-3**.
- Detection unit tests on synthetic frames (patch rules, thresholds, key colour median, HDR tone mapped input), verifies **AC-2**, **AC-3**.

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- Delivers the core product promise with no per take cost and no new vendor.
- Background changes are cheap re-renders that never risk the current video.
- Keying lives in one place (`src/lib/keying.ts`), so AI matting can replace it later without touching data or UI.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Edge quality depends on the creator's lighting; some takes will show faint edges or be skipped.
- Keying makes renders slower: measured in step 1 on 4 vCPUs (the `large-1x` size), a keyed 10 minute 1080p render takes about 15 minutes against 2 minutes plain, so a worst case job (download, trim, detection, render, upload) stays near 20 minutes of the 38.5 minute deadline. Real footage must be timed again before launch.
- Previews need a job run, so they take seconds, and each one reads part of the original.
- Detection thresholds are heuristics that will need tuning with real takes.
- More moving parts in the job layer: a second start path, two new tasks, a seed script.

**Neutral**:
- Re-renders stop sending emails (spec 0006 sent one per finished run).
- `process-take` gains a second mode (the `background` kind) and always renders when a background is chosen.
