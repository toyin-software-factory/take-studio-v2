Follow my voice sends your audio to Deepgram

While you record, your microphone audio is sent live to Deepgram, our speech to text provider, so the prompter can find your place in your script.

Your video, your name and your email are not sent. We don't store this audio, and we ask Deepgram not to use it to train its models.

You can turn it off at any time. The prompter then scrolls on a timer.
